My name is Andrew Plourde and I am a junior at Saint Anselm College. I am a student-athlete double major in Graphic Design and Marketing with the dream of a career in sports media.

I am currently an intern with Falmouth Road Race, Inc. During the summer I am creating designs in preparation for the race including logos, medals, merchandise, signs and more.
